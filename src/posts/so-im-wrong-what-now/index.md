Fight With Your Mind highlights an example of selflessness we often miss. *"Physicists' perspectives could be very important in helping people deprogram from brainwashing. No other group of people are as practiced at having to alter their perceptions of reality than physicists. Their unique experience and expertise could help alleviate the fear associated with letting go of false paradigms in order to get to the truth. Those brave enough to admit that they are wrong need not be scared–physics redefines our reality everyday, and Earth still turns."*
