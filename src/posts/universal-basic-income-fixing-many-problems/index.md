There are several ways to approach fixing the homeless problem, one of which is a universal basic income. Oddly, UBI is discussed more as a poverty solution and less as a homeless solution, likely because homelessness is a complex issue with non-monetary needs. Despite this, a guaranteed income could remove one of the most persistent barriers standing between someone and stability, freeing up resources–both financial and emotional–to address the rest.
