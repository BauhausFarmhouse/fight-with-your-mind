### Fight With Your Mind needs your help!

\[give\_form id="1057" show\_title="false" show\_goal="false" show\_content="none" display\_style="onpage"\]

We are a 100% volunteer organization. Contributors share expertise free of charge because they believe in our mission to fight information warfare by teaching how it is used and why it is effective. Together we can bring a shared reality back into focus by challenging ourselves to explore our own perceptions, question our opinions, and better understand our differences through expertise, education, and empathy.

_We can’t do what we do without you!_

While most of the heavy lifting is done free of charge, Fight With Your Mind has bills to pay. Even small contributions help us cover costs such as hosting, licensing, and other fees associated with keeping the site online.
