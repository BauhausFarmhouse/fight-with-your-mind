### Become a Contributor

Fight With Your Mind welcomes contributor submissions. To support our mission, Fight With Your Mind relies on experts from an array of industries, professions, and public service posts to voice their unique and important knowledge.

_To submit an article, video, audio clip, idea for a story, or a potential "lost cause" to help, send an email to [info@fightwithyourmind.org](mailto:info@fightwithyourmind.org)_

_Required Information to Accompany Submissions_

- Name
- Email
- If you are an individual or represent an organization?
- Briefly describe how your submission relates to Fight With Your Mind
